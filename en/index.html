(01) About me

Music is my life. Now I teach others.

I graduated from the Krzysztof Komeda Music School in Warsaw, and I'm also an engineer – a graduate of AGH University in Kraków. Rock and metal are closest to my heart, but I'm just as happy playing funk and electronic music. As a session musician I record and play with different artists. I've got plenty of albums, concerts all over Poland and abroad, and lots of students under my belt.

In my lessons I pass on what the stage taught me: a solid groove, reading music, playing by ear and the joy of playing in a band.

Outside of music, I'm a dad to an amazing little boy.

(02) Lessons

Lessons tailored to you

I teach in English and Polish. Lekcje także po polsku.

01

For kids

Patient and fun. Rhythm, coordination and their first favourite songs – with lots of fun along the way.

02

For adults

It's never too late. Start from scratch or get back behind the kit after years away, at your own pace.

03

Total beginners

How to hold the sticks, how to sit at the kit, your first rhythms. You'll play a simple beat after the very first lesson.

04

Advanced players

Technique, limb independence, groove, fills, playing to a click and getting ready to play in a band.
